🛡️ Content Governance 1.8.1 is out

Content Governance is the one drawer in jContent and Page Builder that checks a page before it goes live: page quality (accessibility, SEO, speed, readability, eco-design), GEO readiness for AI assistants, and compliance with the site's own glossaries.

What's new in 1.8

  • The drawer docks beside the page. It opens under the header next to Page Builder, like jContent's preview panel, instead of covering the screen. The page stays editable while the drawer is open, and you can drag its edge to make it wider (it remembers the width).
  • Highlight on the page. In Page quality, a finding is outlined right in the page you are editing, so you can click the element and fix it. The separate preview starts hidden in Page Builder, and Show preview brings it back.
  • Works with Page Insights. The drawer moves with the page when the Insights bar above it opens or closes (1.8.1).

Get it: Content Governance on the Jahia Store, for Jahia 8.2 with jContent 3.7 or later.

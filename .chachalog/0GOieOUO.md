Page quality and GEO readiness show each check as a section that opens and closes, instead of a row of tabs. The number of issues of a section sits left of its title, in capitals. A section with a critical or serious issue, or whose check did not finish, opens on its own; the others stay closed. **Expand all** opens every section, and **Open** in **Fix first** opens the section that explains a finding.

The buttons that run a check again, **Re-run**, **Run again** and **Scan again**, are now secondary buttons of the same size as **Export JSON**.

The Page quality dashboard shows what each audit found: expand a page to read the findings of its latest audit, the most serious first, each with what to do about it, its severity, its tab and, for accessibility, how many elements fail the rule. They are read when the row is opened, from the audit stored when an editor ran it, so nothing is audited again and the list stays fast on a site with many pages.

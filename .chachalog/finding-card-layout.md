Simplified the drawer's finding cards and banners: severity is the card's border colour, and only short titles are bold.

A finding card no longer prints its severity as a word: the colour of its left border shows it, and a screen reader still reads it. The **⋮** of its actions ends the title line. The id of its rule sits at the right of the line of its findings, such as `og:image` with `seo.openGraph`. A banner that explains something, such as the note on automated accessibility checks, shows its text in regular weight.

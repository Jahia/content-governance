A tab that another module adds to the drawer can now show as a subdomain of a domain. The module ships a subdomain whose id is the key of its tab, and the drawer shows the tab as the section of that subdomain. On the screen **Rules & domains**, a site administrator moves the tab to another domain or switches it off, as any subdomain, and switches its parts under it. Example: AI Assistant shows **AI review** in Page quality and **Voice** in Compliance.

The page under edit no longer shows through a tab of another module: every tab of the drawer now has an opaque background.
